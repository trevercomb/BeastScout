# BeastScout changelog

## 0.13.16

- Fixed: on some translated clients (seen in Spanish and Russian), a tame could miss the beast's Faster Attack passive, so it was recorded with no attack speed skill and a speed of 2.0. BeastScout now also recognises the passive by its icon, so those tames get the right rank, and pets tamed in the last two weeks are corrected the next time they're out.
- `/beastscout pet` now also shows what BeastScout read from your pet's spellbook, so a passive it couldn't read is easy to spot.
- Starving Mountain Lion is settled again: Faster Attack VI (1.2) and Cower 2. Tames that missed the passive no longer count against the ones that saw it.
- New from players' tames: Elder Shadowmaw Panther (Faster Attack III, Dash 2, Prowl 2), Silt Crawler (Claw 6, Pinch 3), Bellygrub (Charge 3), Venom Web Spider (Web 1), Deepmoss Webspinner (Bite 3), Raging Reef Crawler (Pinch 1), and Foothills Duskbat (Sonic Blast 1), which the Hunter's Handbook never had.
- Hunters like you have now shared skills and speeds for 118 beasts (9 the Handbook never had), plus spawn points for 49 more.

## 0.13.15

- Fixed: since 0.11.0, BeastScout misread Faster Attack ranks on your pet. Faster Attack II and III were recorded a rank low and IV as V, so tames and shared data showed the wrong rank and attack speed. VI and VII, and Slower Attack, were always right.
- Your recorded tames and the shares you've heard are corrected once when you log in with this version. Shares from players still on older versions are corrected as they arrive, so updating is all you need to do. Where the old reading could have been either of two ranks, the Hunter's Handbook decides; if it doesn't know the beast, the reading stays as it was.
- Corrected: Coldrasp, Savannah Huntress, Juvenile Snow Leopard and Vulgara the Insatiable are Faster Attack III (1.5). Mountain Lion, Prairie Wolf Alpha, Prideclaw and Savannah Prowler are Faster Attack IV (1.4). Mystmane stays Faster Attack II (1.6).
- A fresh tame is now checked again after a reload, when you use `/beastscout pet`, and when your pet's spellbook updates, not only after a fight, so a wrong reading gets corrected sooner.
- New from players' tames: Elder Nightsaber (Faster Attack IV), Elmpaw (Claw 2) and Moss Stalker (Web 1).
- Hunters like you have now shared skills and speeds for 111 beasts (8 the Handbook never had), plus spawn points for 52 more.

## 0.13.13

- Right after a tame, the game sometimes shows a lower Faster or Slower Attack rank than the pet really has. BeastScout now remembers the beast each new pet came from, and if the pet's spellbook later shows a different rank within two weeks, it corrects that beast's skills and speed, tells you in chat, and shares the fix.
- Mystmane on Zephras Isle corrected to Faster Attack II (1.6), matching the Hunter's Handbook. Its tame first showed Faster Attack I.
- Your pet readings now include your own stamina and armor, so the measured family bonuses for `/beastscout find tank` can take your gear out once enough readings come in. `/beastscout pet` shows both your pet's and your own current numbers.
- Hunters like you have now shared skills and speeds for 74 beasts (6 the Handbook never had), plus spawn points for 58 more.

## 0.13.8

- With EllesmereUI or ElvUI installed, the BeastScout and export windows are skinned to match, through their skinning APIs. EllesmereUI can turn this off per addon under Blizzard Skins+ > Window Skins > Third-Party Addons. This is new and lightly tested, so please report anything that looks wrong. Without them, nothing changes.
- Sharing is harder to fool. Reports are now votes, with a tame counting more than Beast Lore: one tame still beats one Beast Lore, but a single bad report can no longer outvote several players. A level range far from what other players saw is left out.
- Shared data now replaces your own record only when at least two players saw it themselves.
- Saved shared data has a limit: up to 2000 beasts, dropping the one heard from least recently, and reports nobody has sent for a year are dropped at login.
- Hunters like you have now shared skills and speeds for 54 beasts (5 the Handbook never had), plus spawn points for 58 more.

## 0.12.3

- Fixed: "closest first" put every beast with a known spawn point ahead of beasts known only by zone, so a beast across the continent could come before one in the next zone. Zone-only beasts are now sorted by the middle of their zone.
- Fixed: tamed attack speeds were read from the pet's swing timer, so haste buffs like Might of Stormwind made a normal 2.0 beast record as 1.7. Tames now take their speed from the pet's Faster/Slower Attack rank, and speeds recorded or shared by older versions are corrected the same way.
- Sharing is harder to fool: passed-on reports can no longer push out reports from players who saw a beast themselves, and a speed only heard second-hand now shows as `(shared, passed on)` and counts for less.
- Only what at least two players agreed on is passed on. An attack speed from a single player no longer travels along with agreed abilities.
- Other players' tames now beat your own Beast Lore reading on tooltips, since a tame shows the full, exact list.
- A beast's shared name and family now go by what most players reported, so one odd report can't move a beast to another family in searches.
- `/beastscout help` shows your current virtual realm. Forever's custom chat channels don't cross virtual realms, so live sharing reaches players on the same one; agreed data still reaches everyone in updates.
- Bundled data: Young Forest Bear's speed corrected to 2.5 (Slower Attack II), and Shore Crawler added (2.0, Claw 3, Pinch 1).

## 0.12.2

- Fixed: the minimap button sat inside the minimap when it was bigger than the classic size. It now stays just outside the rim at any size.
- After a game patch, BeastScout says once in chat if the client is newer than the version it was built for, so you know an update may be coming.
- `/beastscout help` says "bundled from players" for the data that ships with BeastScout, to tell it apart from data shared in game.

## 0.12.1

## 0.12.0

- In-game sharing now reaches every BeastScout player online, not just your guild: BeastScout joins a hidden chat channel for it. When you log in, a few players online send what they know so you catch up, kept light however many people use BeastScout. Shown on tooltips as `(shared, tamed x2)`.
- `/beastscout help` shows sharing status: the channel, how many BeastScout players you've heard from, and how many beasts were shared with you.

## 0.11.0

- Remembers each of your pets' skills while it's out, so beast tooltips show upgrades for all your pets, stabled ones included. New **My pets** view in the window, with Remove for pets you've abandoned.

- Shares with your guild in game: guildmates running BeastScout swap their tames and Beast Lore findings automatically, shown on tooltips as `(guild, tamed x2)`. `/beastscout sharing` turns it off.

## 0.10.1

- The export window now says up front that sharing is private: your name isn't kept with your data, and your submission is deleted once it's merged.
- Shared observations now count once per player, so sharing again never double counts (your newer data replaces your older data).

## 0.10.0

First release.

- Beast tooltips: the attack speed your pet will get (from its Faster/Slower Attack passive), the skills it teaches, family abilities and diet, and where each value came from.
- Upgrade hints for your current pet, with training point costs and the pet level a rank needs.
- Your pet's tooltip: loyalty and its level, happiness, and training points, including what the next loyalty step is worth.
- The BeastScout window (/beastscout or the minimap button): upgrades for your pet grouped by skill, the fastest beasts, tank families, and search by skill or family, with map waypoints.
- Reads Beast Training when you open it, so skills you already know say "train it" with their real costs.
- Records your tames and Beast Lore so your own data can improve on the built-in data; share it with /beastscout export.
- Hunters like you have now shared skills and speeds for 6 beasts.
